Many functions are built from small pieces

Learning the small pieces takes far less data than learning the whole function at once.

100,000,000cells for one function of 8 inputs, at 10 levels each
700cells for 7 pieces of 2 inputs

Standard networks miss most of this gain

A network given the target’s graph learns much faster. Dense and sparse baselines stay far behind. LeWi is not given the graph.
