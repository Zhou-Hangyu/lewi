Motivation

Many functions are built from small pieces

Learning the small pieces takes far less data than learning the whole function at once.

100,000,000cells to fill with data for one function of 8 inputs
700cells for 7 pieces of 2 inputs each

At 10 levels per input.

The gap

Standard networks do not find the pieces

A network that is given the target’s graph learns much faster. Dense networks and sparse baselines stay far behind. LeWi is not given the graph.

Results

Fewer samples when the target has structure

The more the target decomposes into small pieces, the more LeWi helps.
